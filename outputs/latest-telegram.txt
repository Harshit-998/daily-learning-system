System Design: Replication
Core idea: Replication is the design tool used when keeping copies of data for reads, locality, and resilience. In interviews, the important part is not the definition; it is deciding where this idea belongs in the request path and what trade-off it creates.
Trade-off: Replication can improve one dimension such as latency, throughput, availability, or operability while increasing another dimension such as consistency complexity or debugging difficulty.

JS Interview: Closures, this-binding, and hidden state
Question: What does a closure actually retain?
Answer: A closure retains references to variables in its lexical environment, not a frozen copy of their values. That is why mutations after function creation can still be observed.

DSA: Tree Transformations and Rerooting
Notice: Tree Transformations and Rerooting usually has a repeated decision that can be made locally once the right state is maintained.
Approach: For Tree Transformations and Rerooting, ask: what information from the past is still relevant, what can be safely discarded, and what condition lets me commit to an answer?
Complexity: The optimized target is usually O(n), O(n log n), or O(states * transitions), depending on the pattern. Space is whatever state is required to preserve the invariant.
